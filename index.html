Our product

Bosca Expenses

Photograph a receipt and Bosca reads the merchant, date, total, VAT and line items for you, ready for an Irish VAT return. Accountants get a web portal where their clients' receipts arrive as they are scanned.

Visit bosca.app

  • Receipt scanning

    Photos or PDFs, one at a time or in a batch. Merchant, date, totals, VAT and line items are read for you.

  • Built for Irish VAT

    VAT rates and classifications that match the Irish return, with a purchases analysis ready for the annual Return of Trading Details.

  • Accountant portal

    Clients' receipts land in their accountant's portal as they are scanned, with review flags and export formats for Sage, Xero, QuickBooks and FreshBooks.
