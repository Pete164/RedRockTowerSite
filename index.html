Red Rock Tower

An Irish software company.

Red Rock Tower Ltd builds software for small businesses in Ireland. Our first product is Bosca Expenses, a receipt scanner made for sole traders, small companies and the accountants who look after them.

What we make

Bosca Expenses

Photograph a receipt and Bosca reads the merchant, date, total, VAT and line items for you, ready for an Irish VAT return. Accountants get a web portal where their clients' receipts arrive as they are scanned.
